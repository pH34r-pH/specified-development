# Qualify a bounded candidate

Adapted from David Zhang's auto-research; see [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md). Use the existing
execution owner and evidence locations under the [entry contract](../SKILL.md).

Separate fixed product acceptance from the plan's optimization objective. Record
baseline identity, evaluator and fixture versions, metric units/direction and
comparison rule, authorized mutable parameters, scope, budgets, stop rules,
required confirmation and promotion permissions before examining candidate scores.
Do not invent a target, weaken a requirement or reinterpret percent as percentage
points. Missing telemetry stays unknown; advisory limits are not verified hard caps.

Choose the smallest inexpensive task that exposes the disputed mechanism. It is a
development loop, not a replacement for final acceptance. Confirm the evaluator
can detect a relevant defect, establish or reuse a compatible baseline, and retain
exact inputs, revision, configuration and raw observations.

Plan a small hypothesis batch: mechanism, predicted result, falsifying observation,
cheapest discriminating test, comparison parent and likely interactions. Change one
conceptual factor per trial where feasible. A necessarily coupled bundle gets a
bundle-level conclusion. Use matched environments, avoid contention and plan noisy
measurement repetition before seeing which repeat looks favorable.

Preserve the incumbent and unrelated work. Record every candidate and failed,
negative, invalid or inconclusive trial through the existing task owner. Repairs
receive a new candidate identity within the authorized repair allowance. A timeout
or infrastructure error is not a zero-valued performance result or a clean review.
Keep changes, evidence and rollback scopes separate so rollback does not erase the
record. A hypothesis plateau calls for revised investigation, not a claim of optimality.

Before selecting a winner, check consumer-level costs: initialization, host crossings,
copies, deployment, memory, recovery and required usability/quality where material.
A faster kernel can be a slower product. Confirm combinations rather than assuming
individual gains add; a more complex combination losing to a simpler candidate does
not become the incumbent.

Keep tuned development cases separate from untouched confirmation cases. Do not
expose reviewer answers or holdout material to the candidate agent. Run accepted
regressions and final required coverage before promotion; do not hide one failed
requirement in an average. Emulator/software-renderer evidence cannot establish
unmeasured device behavior. Preserve all planned attempts when reporting results.

Return retain incumbent, accept candidate, reject candidate or insufficient evidence,
with the exact evidence and scope. Record what was learned, including null results,
not merely a leaderboard. Stop at authorized limits or a material authority gap;
this procedure neither schedules future work nor authorizes a different model,
service, deployment or extended run.
