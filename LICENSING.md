## Required evidence for a candidate release

Release readiness is pending. The [distribution tasks](build/distribution/tasks.md) assign upstream manifest and release-lock oracles to T026, the local component build and `release-lock.json` to T027, and exact candidate qualification to T035. The preset, extension, bundle, and dependency attribution decisions remain pending those candidate archives.

Use the release lock to record each shipped component's source and immutable revision or dependency digest, SPDX identifier, exact archive paths and digests, and required license or notice paths and digests. Classify original, copied, adapted, generated, dependency, and reference-only material by provenance. Generated bytes that carry source material retain that lineage. Record reference-only sources as references and match copied or adapted bytes to their applicable notices. For any shipped Spec Kit material covered by its MIT license, include the applicable copyright and permission notice from the reviewed source.

The local release check must compare each final archive against that reviewed lock, including its license and attribution files. Missing notices, stale revisions or digests, unreviewed paths, and unresolved component license dispositions block release readiness. The [rollout gates](spec/operations/rollout.md) govern publication separately. Track qualification in [issue #17](https://github.com/pH34r-pH/specified-development/issues/17).
